About Me

Music has always been an integral part of my life, blending creativity and expression with a deep sense of connection. I'm a guitarist and songwriter who finds joy in crafting melodies that resonate with perseverance, strength, and resilience.

My musical journey has been shaped by both formal education and personal exploration. I studied music for four years at Northern Vermont University and earned a minor in music from UNC Asheville — deepening my understanding of theory, composition, and performance.

Whether I'm working on a dynamic guitar solo or refining lyrics that linger in the listener's mind, I approach every note with passion and purpose. Music isn't just a hobby; it's a way of life — a way to heal, inspire, and bring people together.
